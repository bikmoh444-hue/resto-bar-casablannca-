About

Relaxed dinners, rooftop evenings, and Moroccan warmth.

Casablanca Restaurant Lounge Bar is a beautifully decorated, comfortable restaurant lounge bar in the centre of Mirleft. Guests come for long relaxed dinners, rooftop seating, Moroccan hospitality, and a proper glass of mint tea.

Enjoy dine-in service or kerbside pickup, with approachable prices and a menu made for lingering over food, drinks, and conversation.
